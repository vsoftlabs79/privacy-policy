Privacy Policy for Space Drift

Effective date: September 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how VSoftLabs (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use the Space Drift mobile game (the “App”).

Information we and our service providers collect

Space Drift does not provide player accounts, sign-in, or a way for players to enter a name, email address, phone number, payment information, or other profile information.

The App uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), Google Analytics for Firebase, Firebase Crashlytics, and Firebase Remote Config. These services may collect and process the following information when you use the App:

  • Device and advertising identifiers: Android Advertising ID, app set ID, Firebase app-instance and installation identifiers, and Crashlytics installation identifiers.
  • Approximate location: your device IP address may be used to estimate general location.
  • App activity: app launches, screens and sessions, interactions with ads, and gameplay events such as game runs, score-related events, selected in-game items, and settings actions.
  • App and device information: app version, operating-system and device information, language, country or region, time zone, network information, Remote Config metadata, and similar technical information.
  • Diagnostics and crash data: app performance information, crash traces, relevant app state, and device metadata used to diagnose and fix problems.

How information is used

We and our service providers use this information to operate and improve the App, configure App and advertising features, measure gameplay and ad performance, serve and measure advertisements, prevent fraud and abuse, and diagnose crashes and technical issues.

AdMob may use device and advertising identifiers, approximate location, app interactions, and diagnostic information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Depending on your device settings, location, and consent choices, ads may be personalized or limited.

Sharing and third-party services

Information collected through the App may be transmitted to Google and its service providers through the SDKs listed below. AdMob may also share relevant information with advertising technology partners to deliver, measure, and protect advertising. These providers process data under their own policies and terms.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Mobile Ads / AdMob
  • Google Analytics for Firebase
  • Firebase Crashlytics and Remote Config
  • Unity Privacy Policy

Advertising choices and consent

Google provides controls that allow you to reset or delete your Android Advertising ID and manage ad personalization through your device settings. Where required by applicable law, we will obtain consent before using information for purposes that require consent. You can also contact us using the details below to ask questions about privacy choices.

Data retention

We do not operate player accounts or maintain a separate player-profile database. Information handled by third-party services is retained according to those providers’ policies and the service configurations that apply to the App. We retain information under our control only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy or as required by law.

Children

Space Drift is not directed to children under 13 years of age, or a higher minimum age where local law requires one. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe that a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will review the request.

Security and international transfers

The third-party services used by the App state that data is encrypted in transit using technologies such as HTTPS or TLS. Information may be processed in countries other than the one where you live, subject to the safeguards and practices described in the relevant provider policies.

Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy to reflect changes to the App, our services, or applicable requirements. We will publish the updated version on this page and revise the effective date.
